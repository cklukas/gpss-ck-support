# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how **GPSS/CK** handles data.

## Summary

- GPSS/CK does not require an account of its own.
- GPSS/CK does not operate a backend service and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer.
- GPSS/CK does not include third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- Models, results and settings stay on your Mac.
- Purchases of the Personal and Professional editions are handled by Apple's App Store.

## Data Stored on Your Mac

GPSS/CK stores information locally so it can work with your files and continue where you left off. This can include:

- your models, input data, run configurations, results and study records, in the files and project folders where you save them
- your settings, such as editor text size, window layout and playback speed
- the list of recently opened documents and projects
- the folders you registered for the app and for the command-line tool `cksim`, kept as permissions that macOS issues for those folders
- a local note of the edition you own, so that `cksim` follows the same edition as the app
- a record of which book examples the app installed into your GPSS/CK folder

This information remains on your Mac and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app's data removes its settings and these records; your own model files are not touched.

## Files and Folders

GPSS/CK runs in the macOS App Sandbox. It can read and write only the documents you open or save and the folders you choose in the system's folder dialog. The command-line tool `cksim` can use only the folders you register for it. You can see and withdraw these folders at any time under **Help > Use GPSS/CK from Terminal…** or with `cksim folders`.

## Purchases

The Personal and Professional editions are purchased through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app keeps only a local note of the edition you own.

## Personal Data

GPSS/CK does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, or Photos library. It does not collect usage data.

## Network

GPSS/CK works offline. The only external service it reaches is Apple's App Store, to show the editions, to make a purchase and to restore purchases.

## Third-Party Services

GPSS/CK does not use third-party analytics, tracking, advertising networks, or user-profiling services.

## Your Choices

You can:

- use GPSS/CK without any purchase and without a network connection
- withdraw any folder you registered, in the app or with `cksim folders remove`
- delete the app at any time; your model files stay where you saved them
